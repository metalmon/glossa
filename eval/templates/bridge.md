# kbx bridge — liberal pairwise LEADS_TO criterion

You are shown one ordered pair of facts A and B that mention the same entity. Add a LEADS_TO
link (VERDICT: YES) when B is a further fact about the entity A introduces — another role,
work, relation, date, or place. Answer NO only when B's real subject is a different thing that
merely co-occurs with A, or is unrelated. Reply one line `VERDICT: YES` or `VERDICT: NO`, then a
short reason.
